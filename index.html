A LETTER FROM MY HEART

To my baby Julie,

Words I wish I could tell you while holding your hand.

My dearest baby Julie, ♡

First of all, I want to remind you how much I love you. You have such a special place in my heart, baby. Whenever I think about you, I find myself smiling for no reason. Even ordinary days become beautiful because I have someone as wonderful as you in my life. If I could choose someone to know and love all over again, I would still choose you.

You are so gorgeous, Julie. And I don't mean only because of your beautiful face and lovely smile. I love your heart, your personality, your laughter, your voice, and the little things you do without realizing how much they mean to me. I love the way you sing for Jesus. Seeing you worship God in Bacolod made me admire you even more. Your voice is beautiful, but seeing your heart for the Lord makes that beauty even more special to me.

Thank you for everything you do for me. Thank you for your time, your patience, your kindness, and all the little things you do to make me happy. Thank you for caring for me even when I don't ask. I notice your efforts, baby, and I appreciate them more than I can explain. I never want you to feel that the things you do for me are unnoticed or unimportant. They mean so much to me.

I am thankful for the moments we have shared, for every conversation, every laugh, and every time I get to hold your hand. Even the simplest moments feel special when I spend them with you. I am also thankful I got to meet your mother. I was shy, baby, but I hope that as time goes on, I can become more comfortable and show my respect for you and your family through my actions.

Baby, I want you to feel safe being yourself around me. You don't have to be perfect to deserve my care. You don't always have to be happy, strong, or ready to talk about everything. When you have a difficult day, I want to listen. When you feel tired, I want to understand. When you feel unsure of yourself, I want to remind you of the beautiful person you are. I want to learn how to understand your heart instead of assuming I know everything you feel.

I want to reassure you that I am serious about the way I care for you. I don't want you to feel like you need to compete with anyone or change who you are to keep my attention. Your past does not take away your worth. I want to appreciate the person you are today and respect the experiences that helped shape you. You deserve patience, honesty, kindness, and a love that makes you feel respected.

I know that love is more than sweet messages, gifts, and saying "I love you." Love also means listening, understanding, respecting boundaries, being honest, and learning from our mistakes. I know I am not perfect, baby, but I want to keep growing. If we misunderstand each other, I hope we can talk with patience. If I make a mistake, I want to take responsibility. I want my actions to show you that your feelings matter.

I also want you to know that you don't have to worry about being exciting every day just to keep me happy. I want the quiet moments with you, too. I want to hear about your day, listen to your random stories, laugh at silly things, and share ordinary moments that become special simply because we are together. I want to keep discovering the little things that make you who you are.

Most importantly, I thank God for allowing our paths to cross. I don't know everything He has planned for us, but I believe we can keep seeking His guidance. I pray that our relationship brings us closer to Jesus and helps us grow in patience, wisdom, humility, and genuine love. I want us to encourage one another in our faith, pray for each other, and remember God in both our happiest moments and our hardest days.

I pray for you, baby. I pray that God protects your beautiful heart, gives you strength when you feel weak, peace when you feel worried, and courage when you face new challenges. I pray for your dreams, your ministry, your family, and everything you hope to achieve. May He continue to use your voice and your heart to bless other people. I hope you always remember how precious you are to Him.

Thank you for being my baby Julie. Thank you for giving me reasons to smile, for making me excited about our next memories, and for letting me become part of your world. I want more conversations, more laughter, more dates, more walks with you, more moments holding your hand, and more opportunities to make you feel appreciated. I want to keep thanking God for the blessings He allows us to experience.

I cannot promise that every day will be easy, and I cannot promise that I will never make mistakes. But I can promise that I will keep learning how to love you with sincerity. I will do my best to listen, respect you, encourage you, and treat your heart with care. I don't want my love to be something you only read in messages. I want you to feel it in the way I treat you every day.

If you ever wonder how much you mean to me, come back to this little letter. Remember that there is someone who admires your beautiful smile, loves hearing your voice, is proud of your heart for Jesus, and thanks God for the opportunity to know you. That someone is me, baby. I am grateful for you in more ways than I can put into words.

I love you, Julie. I love the person you are now, and I look forward to seeing you grow into the person God is shaping you to become. I want to celebrate your victories, comfort you through difficult moments, and keep making memories with you one day at a time.

Whatever tomorrow brings, I hope we continue to choose honesty, patience, faith, and kindness. I hope we keep learning about each other instead of expecting perfection. I want our relationship to be a place where we can be ourselves, communicate with love, and encourage one another to follow God.

Thank you for coming into my life, baby. You are someone I cherish, someone I pray for, and someone I want to keep appreciating. If I could hold your hand right now, I would look at you, smile, and tell you again how thankful I am that I met you.

With all the love in my heart,
Your James ♡
